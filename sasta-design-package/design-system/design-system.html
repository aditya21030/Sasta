Patterns and rules

How screens behave and how numbers are shown.

Tab roots

Home, Basket, Saved and Profile show the floating bottom nav and no back button.

Pushed screens

Results, comparison, details, history and settings pages show a back button and no bottom nav.

One title

Either the header title or a large headline, never both.

One route per control

A destination is reachable from one prominent control per screen. Basket: the persistent bar is the single “Compare basket” action.

Honest numbers

Totals say “Estimated” until fees are known; incomplete baskets say “3 of 4”; unavailable is never free.

Hand-off to retailer

The primary action is “Open Retailer app”. Sasta does not sell groceries.
